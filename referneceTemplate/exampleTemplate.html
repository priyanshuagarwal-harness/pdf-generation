3 · Risks Found
39 risks detected across 10 services — 13 FAIL · 12 WARN · 14 INFO. Each risk listed with description and
affected services.
FAIL
Single Replica
Service runs with only one pod instance. Any crash,
eviction, or node failure causes complete
unavailability with no automatic recovery or failover.
Affects: paymentservice · cartservice · checkoutservice
· currencyservice · emailservice
FAIL
No PodDisruptionBudget (PDB)
No PDB configured. Kubernetes may evict all pods
simultaneously during node drains or cluster
maintenance, creating full-service outage windows.
Affects: paymentservice · cartservice · checkoutservice
FAIL
Missing Health Probes
No liveness or readiness probes defined.
Kubernetes cannot detect unhealthy containers,
preventing automatic restarts and routing them live
traffic while broken.
Affects: paymentservice
FAIL
Missing Resource Limits
No CPU/memory limits set. Containers can consume
all node resources, triggering OOM-kill cascades
and noisy-neighbour failures across co-located pods.
Affects: paymentservice · cartservice · currencyservice
· recommendationservice
WARN
No Circuit Breaker
No circuit breaker pattern implemented. Downstream
failures cascade upstream unchecked, causing
latency spikes and eventual full-chain service
failures.
Affects: paymentservice · checkoutservice ·
currencyservice
WARN
No HorizontalPodAutoscaler (HPA)
No HPA configured. Service cannot auto-scale under
increased load, leading to degraded performance or
outage during traffic spikes.
Affects: checkoutservice · recommendationservice
WARN
Missing Resource Requests
No resource requests set. The Kubernetes scheduler
makes suboptimal placement decisions, risking
co-location with resource-hungry pods.
